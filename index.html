16-Week Course Curriculum

Right-click any week to configure its Canvas link
Week 1

Intro to EMS Systems

Well-being, legal, ethical & workforce safety.
Week 2

Medical, Legal, & Ethics

Consent, refusal, negligence & documentation.
Week 3

Human Body & Lifespan

Anatomy basics, physiology & developmental stages.
Week 4

Lifting & Moving Patients

Body mechanics, equipment & emergency drags.
Week 5

Patient Assessment

Scene size-up, primary & secondary assessment.
Week 6

Airway Management

Respiration, airway adjuncts & oxygen therapy.
Week 7

Pharmacology & Med Admin

Routes, medications carried & administration.
Week 8

Midterm Review & Exam

Comprehensive practical and written assessment.
Week 9

Cardiology Emergencies

ACS, chest pain, AED & cardiac arrest protocols.
Week 10

Respiratory Emergencies

Asthma, COPD, dyspnea & specialized inhalers.
Week 11

Neurological Emergencies

Stroke, altered mental status, seizures & syncope.
Week 12

Gastrointestinal & Urologic

Abdominal pain, GI bleeding & renal failure.
Week 13

Trauma & Bleeding Control

Soft tissue injuries, hemorrhage & shock management.
Week 14

Orthopedic & Spinal Trauma

Fractures, dislocations, splinting & spinal immobilization.
Week 15

Special Populations & OB/GYN

Childbirth, pediatric & geriatric emergencies.
Week 16

Operations & Final Prep

EMS operations, triage, Hazmat & NREMT board prep.
